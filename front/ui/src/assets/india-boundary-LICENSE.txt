Why this file exists
--------------------
The vendored world basemap (world-countries-110m.json, Natural Earth 110m)
draws national boundaries on lines of de-facto control. For India that omits
Aksai Chin and parts of Jammu & Kashmir, and treats Arunachal Pradesh as
disputed. LGD is India's own authoritative administrative source, so a boundary
dissolved from it is correct by construction.

Verified before use against the official extent:
    this file   68.178..97.413 E   6.753..37.088 N
    official    68.117..97.417 E   8.067..37.100 N
(The southern figure reaches past the mainland because Andaman & Nicobar are
included.)

Simplified with Douglas-Peucker at 0.035 degrees, from 756 polygons /
317,369 points to 7 / 1,036. That tolerance is far finer than any zoom level
this map offers resolves, and the simplification does not move the boundary
outward anywhere.

Natural Earth remains in use for every other country and for the landmass.
